Beam Monte Carlo

Suggested pace: 30 seconds.

The sweep moved the mean incidence. At each chosen mean angle, we also average incident directions and weighted wavelengths using Monte Carlo or quasi-Monte Carlo source states. Each has its own Ewald sphere, and the footprint projects onto the detector. Wavelength spread strongly affects the radial extension near 003, while mosaicity affects the transverse width. Structure factors, finite thickness and optical corrections supply the intensity weights.
