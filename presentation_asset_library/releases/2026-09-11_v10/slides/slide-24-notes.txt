Stacking disorder and polytypes

Suggested pace: 35 seconds.

Radial Q measures in-plane momentum transfer. Qz is the component along the mean film normal. We extended Hendricks–Teller using transition matrices. Changing their probabilities broadens ordered peaks into diffuse intensity. Next the parent matrices stay fixed while their populations change, adding maxima from different repeats. The fifty, twenty-five, twenty-five mixture is illustrative. Those are population fractions, not layer-transition probabilities. The fitted B4 example that follows uses 2H and both handed 6H parents.

Sources and interpretation
The movie starts directly in normalized Q_R/Q_z coordinates. Q_R is radial momentum transfer in the mean-film frame; Q_z is its normal component. Both axes use the original schematic r=1 ring radius as one unit. The separate detector-coordinate morph is backup slide 22.
N=72 equal-unit-amplitude trilayers, fixed Gaussian-like mosaic, source and detector acceptance. The evolving six-state transition matrix reaches five allowed probabilities of 0.2 at epsilon=0.8. The endpoint retains allowed-stacking constraints and is not an amorphous or fully uncorrelated limit.
The matrix layout and probability scale stay fixed. The second stage changes stack populations to General 0%, 2H 50%, 6H 25%, 4H 25%. These are illustrative population fractions, distinct from per-interface transition probabilities. Both handed variants have equal weights within each illustrated family. The subsequent saved B4 comparison contains 2H plus 6H+ and 6H-, with no 4H component.
