Ewald intersections and mosaic spread

Suggested pace: 20 seconds.

For one incident direction and wavelength, the Ewald sphere is fixed. Only features on its surface satisfy elastic scattering. Mosaic tilt spreads the reciprocal features into caps and bands, creating extended intersections. We now follow those selected traces to the detector.
