Changing incidence

Suggested pace: 30 seconds.

Now change the mean incidence for Bi₂Se₃ from one to twenty degrees. The wavelength stays fixed, so the Ewald sphere keeps its radius while its center moves. The flashes mark exact 003, 006 and 009 crossings. Both views include the nine selected reflections. The fifty-percent Lorentzian weight is illustrative and remains fixed. We next average over the beam’s distribution of directions and wavelengths.

Sources and interpretation
Actual Bi2Se3 geometry: a=4.143 A, c=28.636 A, R-3m, one Cu Kalpha1 wavelength 1.540592925 A. Mean incidence 1 to 20 degrees. The nine shown positive-L reflections are 003/006/009, 101/104/107 and 012/015/018. Paired amber flashes mark exact cap-center crossings at 4.6287, 9.2880 and 14.0103 degrees.
The spherical mosaic density is illustrative: sigma=4.3 degrees, gamma=6 degrees, Lorentzian fraction=0.50 (latest revision). No fitted parameter is changed. Angular image has fixed contrast and omits structure-factor weighting, detector acceptance, substrate exit masking and detector-count normalization. The preceding generic animation ends at 65 percent in different schematic geometry; this is a separate actual-lattice example, not a continuous parameter handoff.
