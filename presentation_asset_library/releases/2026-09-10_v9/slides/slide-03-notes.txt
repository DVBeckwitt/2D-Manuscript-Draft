Many crystallites, one pattern

Suggested pace: 25 seconds.

A real film contains many crystallites simultaneously. Different in-plane angles populate the rings. Different normal tilts spread the reciprocal features into bands and caps. The enlarged view makes that tilt visible. These orientations coexist in a stationary sample. We fit indexed positions first, before interpreting the widths.
