Mosaic tails in angular coordinates

Suggested pace: 35 seconds.

The same detector pattern can be expressed in scattering angle and azimuth. Follow the labeled features into this chart. Each reciprocal shell becomes a vertical trace at fixed scattering angle. Now keep the beam and component widths fixed while increasing the Lorentzian weight. Probability moves into broader tails along azimuth. These values are illustrative.
